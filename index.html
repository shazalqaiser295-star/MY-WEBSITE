Common questions

Before we get started.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No. Search rankings depend on many factors outside any provider’s control. The focus is on sound SEO work, measurable tasks and transparent reporting.

Are these one-time or monthly services?

On-page and technical SEO plans are one-time project scopes. Off-page SEO plans are monthly.

Do you work with international businesses?

Yes. Projects can be discussed with businesses in Pakistan and other markets.

How does payment work?

Project scope and payment arrangements are discussed directly before work begins. There is no online checkout on this page.

Are backlinks guaranteed?

No. Outreach and placements depend on publisher relevance, editorial review and acceptance. Any third-party costs are discussed in advance.

Can I request a custom plan?

Yes. Share your website and goals and we can discuss a suitable scope.
